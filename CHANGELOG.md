### Added

- Settings tab with a **Writing goals** section: story folder, words/characters
  metric, a daily writing goal, and a Monday-first writing-days picker. The
  weekly and monthly goals are calculated from those and shown below the picker
  (the monthly figure counts the writing days that actually fall in the current
  calendar month). A placeholder **Stats** section is stubbed for later work.
